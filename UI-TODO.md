# PREMORTEM — UI/UX problems to fix

## Results page
1. Too long, and every section looks equally important; the answer is buried.
2. "The odds" repeats what the Risk Score already says.
3. Checklist comes before "How your idea could fail"; it reads better after.
4. Risk breakdown shows 5 red bars at once: alarming and noisy.
5. "We haven't found how surviving companies handled this yet" repeats 3 times.
6. Extra "PREMORTEM" label above the idea (the logo is already there).

## Home page
7. No proof of what's inside (e.g. "1,000+ companies · 116 business types · updated nightly").
8. The 3 "how it works" lines are generic; a mini preview of a real result would sell it better.

## Everywhere
9. Light grey text is too faint to read comfortably.
10. No link preview when shared (WhatsApp/LinkedIn) and no browser-tab icon.
11. Loading screen is bare; needs step-by-step progress with ticks.

## Plan
- Round 1 (results): summary card at top · menu that stays on screen · new order · remove duplicates · fold breakdown · hide filler lines
- Round 2 (home + loading): live graveyard stats · mini result preview · step-by-step loading
- Round 3 (polish): darker grey text · tab icon · share preview card · footer · refreshed PDF with risk score and red flags
