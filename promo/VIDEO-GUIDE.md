## What made your reference video so hooking (and how we used each idea)

| Trick in the reference | What it is | How premortem's videos use it |
|---|---|---|
| A little character that leads your eye | **Mascot / guide** | The orange **heartbeat line** is premortem's character: it beats, flatlines, zips in and presses the button, and comes back green |
| "Another **API?**" — grey word then bold word | **Two-tone kinetic typography** | "*Every startup* **has a plan.**", "*Most never run a* **premortem.**" |
| Words blur in, one by one | **Blur-rise text reveal** | Every headline |
| Cream ↔ black hard cuts on the beat | **Beat-synced cuts** | Cream for hope, night for the graveyard, orange for "Free." |
| Tilted code flying past + counter rolling 10 → 15411 | **Speed + motion blur + number roll** | Tilted wall of real headlines + "912 news articles cited" rolling up |
| Typing in a box, then "Press." | **UI demo with an impact word** | Idea typed in, the heartbeat dot presses the button → "Run." |
| Cards flicking past | **Deck / montage** | Real tombstones dropping in, one per beat (Pets.com, Juicero, Byju's…) |
| One-line slogans, one per beat | **Rapid-fire taglines** | "No source, / no company. / Nothing made up. / Free." |
| Logo + tagline + URL | **End card** | Tombstone logo builds itself, then the tagline and a button |
| Clicks, whooshes, booms on every move | **Sound design** | Heart thumps, monitor beeps, the flatline tone, thuds, whooshes, a riser, a chime |

**The 5 rules behind "hooking":**
1. **Hook in 2 seconds.** Start mid-action with a provocative line ("Every startup has a plan.").
2. **Change something every 0.5–1.5s** — a cut, a word, a zoom. The eye never rests.
3. **Cut on the beat.** Pick a tempo (80–110 BPM) and put every change on a beat.
4. **One idea per shot**, big type, few words.
5. **Sound sells motion.** Every movement gets a whoosh, click, thud or pop.

## How these were made (the technical bit, simply)

1. **The animation is a web page.** `video.html` + `engine.js` draw each frame (text, heartbeat line, tombstones, rings, bars) for any moment in time. Each video's script is `v1.js`, `v2.js`, `v3.js`.
2. **A robot browser takes a photo of every frame** — 30 photos per second (`render.cjs`).
3. **The soundtrack is generated by code** (`sound.py`): it reads the list of "sound cues" each video wrote (e.g. *beep at 1.2s, thud at 9.5s*) and builds music + effects exactly on time.
4. **The Mac's built-in video tools stitch photos + sound into an MP4** (`encode.swift`).

### To change a video and rebuild it (ask Claude to do this for you)
```
cd ~/Desktop/who-tried-it/promo
python3 -m http.server 8766 &           # serve the animation page
node render.cjs 1                        # photos of every frame (video 1)
python3 sound.py 1                       # soundtrack
afconvert -f m4af -d aac -b 256000 audio1.wav audio1.m4a
./encode frames1 30 audio1.m4a premortem-video-1-before-you-build.mp4
```

## Where to get tools to make videos like this yourself

**Easiest (no code, drag and drop)**
- **Jitter** (jitter.video) — made for exactly this style: kinetic text, UI animations, beat-synced. Free plan. ⭐ best fit for you.
- **CapCut** (desktop, free) — editing, auto-captions, beat markers, sound effects library.
- **Canva** video — quick social versions, templates.
- **Rive** (rive.app) — animated mascots/characters that you can also put on the website.

**Professional**
- **Adobe After Effects** — the industry standard for motion graphics (paid).
- **DaVinci Resolve** (free) — editing + colour + sound (Fairlight).
- **Figma + Jitter** — design screens in Figma, animate in Jitter.

**Code-based (what we used — good for data-driven videos)**
- **Remotion** (remotion.dev) — make videos with React code.
- **Motion Canvas** (motioncanvas.io) — code-driven animation, great for explainers.

## Where to get sounds and music
- **Pixabay Music & Sound Effects** (pixabay.com/sound-effects) — free, no attribution.
- **Freesound** (freesound.org) — huge free SFX library (check each licence).
- **Epidemic Sound / Artlist** — paid, very high quality, safe for social media.
- **Splice** — loops and one-shots for music beds.
- Search terms that give this "tech promo" sound: *UI click, whoosh, swoosh, impact hit, riser, glitch, typing, heartbeat, monitor beep, sub drop*.

## Where to learn
- **YouTube:** "Jitter tutorial", "kinetic typography After Effects", "Ben Marriott" (After Effects motion design, beginner-friendly), "School of Motion" (free tutorials), "Remotion tutorial".
- **Courses:** School of Motion (*Animation Bootcamp*), Motion Design School.
- **Inspiration:** Jitter's template gallery, Dribbble/Behance search "product motion", Apple and Linear product videos, the "Made with Remotion" showcase.
- **Practice tip:** copy one 5-second shot you love, frame by frame. That's how motion designers learn.

## How to come up with ORIGINAL ideas (not copies of other videos)

1. **Start from premortem's own world**, not other ads: medicine (heart monitor, X-ray, autopsy), death (graveyard, obituary, museum), time (rewind), evidence (clippings, red string).
2. **Pick ONE strict rule** for the whole video — e.g. "one unbroken camera glide" (video 4), "everything drawn by one line", "everything is an X-ray".
3. **Borrow from other art forms**: museum exhibits, weather reports, film title sequences (search "Saul Bass"), documentaries, risograph posters, stop-motion paper.
4. **Pick a fresh texture**: 3D gallery, paper cut-outs, blueprint, X-ray glow, a single ink line.
5. **Test it**: Mute test (works without sound?) · Screenshot test (is any frame beautiful alone?) · Swap test (if another brand could use it, it's not original yet).

**Video 4 — "Hall of Shut-Downs"** follows this: a quiet 3D museum where each room is a real cause of death, a sculpture under a spotlight (a falling coin tower, a pawn beside a giant king, a knot that can't be untied, an empty chair), real plaques with real companies, and an empty plinth at the end: *"Your idea isn't in here. Yet."* Sound: footsteps with hall echo, spotlight clunks, soft bell notes, a low drone.

**Other original concepts ready to build next:** *One Line* (whole video drawn by one orange line) · *The Scan* (X-ray / MRI of a pitch deck) · *Startup Forecast* (a weather report for business types) · *Paper Trail* (newspaper cut-outs + red string) · *Rewind* (a shut-down office running backwards in time).
